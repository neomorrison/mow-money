# Copy rules

The interface speaks as itself, in the shortest label that makes sense.

1. Say what the player does, not how the software works. "Knock", "Hire", "End Day".
2. Plain verbs: Buy, Sell, Hire, Assign, Mow, Knock, Offer, Accept, Walk away.
3. Do not explain obvious interface behavior.
4. Friendly means clear, not chatty. No "you're all set", "let's get started", "here's where you can".
5. Do not repeat the heading in the body.
6. One vocabulary: a person who pays you is a "client", a house you could win is a "lead" only when warm, otherwise "house". Money is "cash". Stars are "reputation".
7. No narrator voice in system text.
8. State problems plainly: "Not enough cash.", "Your trailer is full.", "Closed until 7:30 AM."
9. Cut filler words: just, simply, all, every, your own.
10. Fragments are fine.

Hard rules: no em dashes anywhere (use periods, commas, colons or parentheses), no emoji in UI chrome, no exclamation marks in system text. Homeowners and staff are characters: their dialogue can be warm, funny, grumpy and may use an exclamation mark when it fits the character.
